# Interview Bank — Foundations (Section A)

Promote more questions as Weeks 1–4 progress.

## Basic
1. What is Generative AI? How is it different from traditional ML?
2. Order AI, ML, DL, GenAI, LLM from broadest to narrowest.
3. Discriminative vs generative models — one example each.
4. What does an LLM do in one sentence?

## Intermediate
5. Why do LLMs hallucinate?
6. Why might next-token prediction produce different answers to the same question?
7. When would you *not* use an LLM alone?

## Scenario
8. Manager asks to use ChatGPT alone for exact payroll for 5,000 employees. What do you say?

## Common mistakes
- Saying "LLMs look up facts in a database"
- Confusing ML with GenAI
- Claiming LLMs always tell the truth
